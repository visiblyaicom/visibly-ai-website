## What is an AEO plugin for WordPress?

An **AEO plugin for WordPress** is a tool that helps you optimize your content for AI answer engines — systems like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews that pull answers directly from web content rather than returning a list of links.

Traditional SEO plugins (Yoast, Rank Math) optimize for Google rankings. An AEO plugin optimizes for AI citation: whether your content gets pulled as the answer when someone asks an AI engine a question related to your topic.

## Why do WordPress sites need a separate AEO plugin?

SEO plugins were built for a world where the goal was to rank on Google's search results page. The signals that matter for that — backlinks, keyword density, meta tags — are different from the signals that matter for AI citation.

AI engines don't rank pages. They extract answers. They look for content that is structured in a specific way: question-format headings, short direct answers, FAQ schema, defined terms, clear document hierarchy. Standard SEO plugins don't score or guide you on these signals.

An AEO plugin fills that gap. It tells you whether your content is structured in a way that AI engines can actually use — and what to fix when it isn't.

## What signals does an AEO plugin check?

A good AEO plugin checks the structural elements that determine whether AI engines can extract and cite your content. The core signals are:

### Question-format headings

AI engines answer questions. They look for content organized the same way — H2s and H3s phrased as questions ("What is X?" rather than "Overview of X"). Content with declarative headings forces AI engines to guess what question the section answers.

### Short direct answers

The first sentence under each question heading should answer the question directly in 40–100 words. AI engines pull this paragraph as the answer. Burying the answer inside a long paragraph makes the content harder to extract.

### FAQ schema markup

**FAQ schema** is structured data that lists your questions and answers in a machine-readable format. AI engines can parse it directly without reading the full page. It is one of the highest-leverage signals for AI citation.

### Defined terms

Bolded definitions early in the article — "**AEO** is the practice of…" — signal authority and get cited heavily. Glossary-style content is a format AI engines are specifically designed to extract.

### Article schema, meta description, heading hierarchy, word count

Supporting signals that collectively tell AI engines your content is fresh, authoritative, and well-structured. See the [full breakdown of all 10 AEO signals](/blog/10-signals-ai-citation) for details.

## How does an AEO plugin work in WordPress?

The best AEO plugins integrate directly into the Gutenberg editor so you can see your score as you write — similar to how Yoast shows an SEO score in the sidebar.

When you open a post, the plugin scores your content on each AEO signal and shows you which ones you're passing and failing. You can edit your content and watch the score update in real time, fixing issues before you publish.

Some AEO plugins also offer AI-powered suggestions — where an AI reads your post and tells you specifically what to rewrite to improve each failing signal, with copy-pasteable fixes ready to drop in.

## Does an AEO plugin replace an SEO plugin?

No. AEO and SEO plugins serve different purposes and you should use both.

**SEO plugin (Yoast, Rank Math):** optimizes for Google rankings — keyword targeting, sitemap generation, meta tag management, technical SEO.

**AEO plugin (Visibly AI):** optimizes for AI citation — content structure, schema markup, llms.txt, AI-specific signals.

The two tools are complementary. Think of your SEO plugin as the foundation and your AEO plugin as the AI search layer on top.

## How long does it take to AEO-optimize a WordPress post?

For a new post written with AEO in mind from the start, 10–15 minutes of additional structure work. For an existing post, 20–30 minutes to rework headings, add direct answers, and attach schema markup.

The fastest wins are always: convert declarative headings to questions, add a short direct answer under each heading, and attach FAQ schema to any post that has a question-and-answer structure.
